    Load the samples or add your own files on the left. Then you get three things:

    1. Who owes what, by when. Every commitment with its clause, and the ones an amendment replaced, struck through.
    2. Answers with quotes. Ask in plain words. If nothing in the documents supports an answer, it says so.
    3. A check of one document against another. Dates, amounts and places that disagree, each with both quotes.

    Before you rely on it

    Not legal advice. ClaimTrace is a reading aid: check every quote before you act on it.

    On amendment wording it had not seen before, its amendment check was wrong about 4 times in 10 (verdict accuracy 0.60 on 105 checks). It usually errs by saying “needs review” where a change was real. The numbers, with every case.

    This browser version uses the rules engine only: no language model, no OCR for scanned PDFs.

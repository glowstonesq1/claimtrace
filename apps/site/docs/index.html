In plain words

ClaimTrace reads contracts, amendments and reports. It lists who owes what and by when, notices when an amendment replaces a clause, and checks one document against another.

It never answers from memory. Every result carries a quote from your documents, and the quote is checked against the source text character for character. When nothing supports an answer, it says so.

It is a reading aid, not legal advice. Its weakest spot is amendment wording it has not seen before, where it is wrong about 4 times in 10.

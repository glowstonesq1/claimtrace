1920x1080, 30 fps, 50.3 s. One continuous camera world, no cuts, no fades to black. Must look like the v0.4 site (apps/site): dusk band with a CSS evening sky, smoked glass panel with a lit top edge, near-white paper sheet with hairlines, the lanes-and-connectors evidence map. Palette from site tokens (oklch). Instrument Sans for display, Figtree for reading text, JetBrains Mono only for clause numbers.

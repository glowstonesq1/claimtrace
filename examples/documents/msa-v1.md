# Master Services Agreement

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into on January 10, 2026 between Halden Freight Co. (the "Customer") and Corvane Software Ltd. (the "Vendor"). This is a fictional document created for ClaimTrace examples.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "Phase 1 System" means the route-planning software described in Schedule A.

1.2 "Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in England.

1.3 "Acceptance" means written confirmation by the Customer that the Phase 1 System meets the acceptance criteria in Schedule A.

## 2. Delivery

2.1 The Vendor shall deliver the Phase 1 System no later than March 31, 2026.

2.2 The Vendor shall provide weekly written progress reports to the Customer until Acceptance.

2.3 The Customer shall provide the Vendor with access to its test environment within 10 Business Days of the date of this Agreement.

## 3. Fees and Payment

3.1 The Customer shall pay the Vendor a fixed fee of GBP 120,000 for the Phase 1 System.

3.2 The Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within 30 days of receipt.

3.3 The Vendor may charge interest on late payments at 4% per year above the Bank of England base rate.

## 4. Service Levels

4.1 The Vendor shall ensure the Phase 1 System is available 99.5% of the time in each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance.

4.2 If availability falls below 99.5% in any month, the Customer is entitled to a service credit of 5% of the monthly support fee.

## 5. Security and Data

5.1 The Vendor shall encrypt all Customer data at rest using AES-256 or stronger.

5.2 The Vendor shall not transfer Customer data outside the United Kingdom without the Customer's prior written consent.

5.3 The Vendor shall notify the Customer of any personal data breach within 48 hours of becoming aware of it.

5.4 The Vendor shall maintain multi-factor authentication for all administrative access to systems that process Customer data.

## 6. Delay

6.1 If the Vendor fails to deliver the Phase 1 System by the date in clause 2.1, the Vendor shall pay liquidated damages of GBP 1,000 for each day of delay, up to a maximum of GBP 30,000.

6.2 Clause 6.1 does not apply where the delay is caused by the Customer's failure to comply with clause 2.3.

## 7. Term and Termination

7.1 This Agreement continues for 24 months from the date of this Agreement unless terminated earlier.

7.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving 90 days' written notice to the other party.

7.3 The Customer may terminate this Agreement immediately if the Vendor commits a material breach that is not remedied within 30 days of written notice.

## 8. Confidentiality

8.1 Each party must keep the other party's confidential information secret and must not disclose it to any third party, except as required by law.

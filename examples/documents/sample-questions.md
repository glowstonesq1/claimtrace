|---|---|---|
| Did the vendor promise delivery by March 31? | stated (but superseded) | MSA 2.1 says March 31; Amendment 1.1 replaces it with May 15. |
| What are the vendor's obligations? | stated | Clauses 2.1, 2.2, 4.1, 5.1–5.4, Amendment 1.4. |
| What happens if the vendor misses the delivery deadline? | stated | MSA 6.1 liquidated damages, 6.2 exception. |
| Does the audit report support that encryption at rest is implemented? | stated | Audit 2.1. |
| Is customer data kept in the UK? | contradicted | MSA 5.2 vs Audit 2.2 (Dublin). |
| What is the liability cap? | not_addressed | No limitation-of-liability clause. |
| How many days does the customer have to pay invoices? | stated | 45 days after Amendment 1.2 (was 30). |

# Amendment No. 1 to the Master Services Agreement

This Amendment No. 1 is made on February 20, 2026 between Halden Freight Co. (the "Customer") and Corvane Software Ltd. (the "Vendor") and amends the Master Services Agreement dated January 10, 2026 (the "Agreement"). This is a fictional document created for ClaimTrace examples.

## 1. Amendments

1.1 Clause 2.1 of the Agreement is deleted and replaced with: "The Vendor shall deliver the Phase 1 System no later than May 15, 2026."

1.2 Clause 3.2 of the Agreement is deleted and replaced with: "The Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within 45 days of receipt."

1.3 Clause 5.3 of the Agreement is deleted and replaced with: "The Vendor shall notify the Customer of any personal data breach within 72 hours of becoming aware of it."

1.4 The following clause is added as clause 5.5: "The Vendor shall complete an independent penetration test of the Phase 1 System before go-live and share the summary report with the Customer."

## 2. Fees

2.1 In consideration of the revised delivery date, the Vendor shall reduce the fixed fee in clause 3.1 to GBP 110,000.

## 3. General

3.1 Except as amended by this Amendment, the Agreement remains in full force and effect.

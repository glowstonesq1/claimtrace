# Plan: v0.2 release, launch site, promo film (2026-11-18)

Spec: docs/roadmap.md (v0.2 and v1.0 sections), docs/design-review.md (D1–D24), docs/evaluation.md (protocol).

## Global constraints
- Floor rules from v0.1 hold: every surfaced claim is a verified quote; nothing leaves the machine by default;
  no invented metrics, testimonials or logos anywhere (site, film, README).
- Eval integrity: the new amendment holdout (W1) is written blind and committed BEFORE engine work is scored;
  the engine workstream (W2) never opens `evals/datasets/amendments-v2/`.
- `make check` (ruff, mypy, pytest) and the web e2e smoke stay green on every branch.
- Motion: transform/opacity only, `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback, content visible without JS.

## Workstreams (parallel, one branch each, merged into release/v0.2)
| ID | Branch | Owner | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| W1 | ws/amend-holdout | blind case-writer agent | evals/datasets/amendments-v2 (>=40 sealed cases) |
